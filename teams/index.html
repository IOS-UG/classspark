Why teachers team up this way

Grouping students by hand costs ten minutes and always leaves someone grumbling about "unfair teams." This generator shuffles the whole class with a genuine random shuffle and deals students around the teams round-robin — so sizes stay balanced and nobody ever needs to argue with the method. Group your class for labs, literature circles, project sprints, centers, or review games — then press Print for a clean page to stick on the wall.

Can I avoid certain students being together?

This generator keeps grouping fast and fair. For special pairings, use it as a starting point and swap names in the copy-pasted list — most teachers find the random result already works.

Does it remember my class?

Your list can be saved by name in this browser (e.g. "Period 2"). It never leaves your device — no account, no upload.
