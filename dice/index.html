Using dice in class

Math warm-ups: roll 2d6 and race to add, multiply, or build fractions. Probability lessons: collect a history of d6 rolls and graph the distribution in front of everyone — real data in one click. Games & review: dice decide how many points a team can earn or how many squares a token moves. And d20 is the teacher's secret chaos engine: press a student's luck in seconds (pair it with the wheel spinner for the class list).

Does it work offline?

Once the page is loaded, it runs entirely in your browser — like every ClassSpark tool. Spotty classroom Wi-Fi won't stop your game.
