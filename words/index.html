What reading time means here

Reading time assumes average silent reading at ~200 words per minute; speaking time assumes a comfortable presentation pace of ~130 words per minute. Teachers set essay length limits, reading-in-turn segments and speech practice around these numbers; students check their essay rubrics ("500 words, 2 paragraphs") before submitting. Nothing you write here is sent anywhere — it's counted by your own browser, instantly.
