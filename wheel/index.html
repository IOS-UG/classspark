How teachers use the wheel

Fair cold-calling: every student knows the wheel decides — no hands, no favorites. Turn on Auto-remove winners and every student gets a turn before the wheel refills. Brain breaks: put activities on the wheel instead of names and let the class spin for the next game. Presentations: spin to pick the next presenter. All quiet on the front: project it fullscreen on the smartboard — the class watches the wheel slow down together.

Is the pick really random?

Yes — the wheel uses your browser's random number generator to choose the winning segment before the animation, then slows down exactly onto it. Every entry has an equal chance.

Can I give some students a higher chance?

Add the same name more than once — repeated names appear on extra segments, so some students truly do get two or three shots. Teachers use this for extra motivation.

Does it work on a smartboard / tablet?

Yes. Open this page on any device, tap SPIN — no app, no install, no Wi-Fi login forms. The page works with or without a keyboard.
