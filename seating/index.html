Why a random seating chart works

The start-of-term battle: separate the chatty pair without hurting feelings, and make sure nobody is always cornered. This maker deals a fresh random layout that nobody can blame you for — then you drag any seat to fine-tune it around real needs (sightlines, helpful neighbors, empty desks). New random layout every few weeks keeps the room fresh and student groups mixed.

Can I leave seats empty?

Yes — an empty grid cell is simply a free desk. Drag anyone back to the “Unassigned” box to free their seat, or don't assign them yet.

Does it remember my layout between visits?

Yes — your last chart and any saved class lists stay in this browser on your own device. No account, no uploads.
